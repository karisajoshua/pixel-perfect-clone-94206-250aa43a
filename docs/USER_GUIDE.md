# Agency staff guide — initial source-based edition

This guide is a navigation reference, **not yet a screenshot-verified operating manual**.

## Sign in
Open the application sign-in page and enter your email address or telephone number and password. If your account has a verified TOTP factor requiring a step-up, complete the verification challenge. Depending on your membership and role, the application routes you to the agency dashboard, customer portal or onboarding.

## Agency workspace
- **Clients:** browse and manage customer records.
- **Quotations:** create and review quotations; the source includes PDF generation and a quotation-to-policy conversion action.
- **Policies:** access policy records.
- **Claims:** record incidents and track claim status.
- **Invoices:** view billing records.
- **Renewals:** filter expiring policies by overdue, seven days, 30 days or 31–60 days.
- **Reports:** access reporting.
- **Assistant:** ask insurance-related questions; verify any generated financial or coverage advice before acting.

## Customer portal
Separate routes exist for customer policies, claims, invoices, documents, vehicles and profile.

## Safety
Do not upload real customer information into test environments. Never rely solely on AI output for binding cover, settling a claim or confirming payment. Ask an administrator before modifying financial records.
